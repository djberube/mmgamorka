# 🐱 Space Cats: Mining for Josh - The Ultimate Edition 🚀

An epic browser-based space mining and strategy game featuring cats named Josh who mine space rocks across the galaxy!

## 🎮 Game Overview

Space Cats combines elements from multiple legendary games:
- **Master of Orion 2** - 4X strategy (Explore, Expand, Exploit, Exterminate)
- **Monopoly** - Property acquisition and territory control
- **Raft** - Crafting and resource management
- **The Long Dark** - Survival mechanics
- **Age of Empires 2** - RTS combat and unit management
- **Pac-Man** - Maze navigation and ghost chasing

## 🎯 Objective

Build your space cat empire by:
- Mining valuable space rocks
- Recruiting and managing an army of Joshes
- Expanding your territory across the galaxy
- Researching advanced technologies
- Crafting powerful items
- Defeating enemy factions
- Surviving in the harsh void of space

## 🕹️ Controls

### Keyboard
- **WASD / Arrow Keys** - Move your Josh cat
- **Space Bar** - Attack nearest enemy
- **ESC** - Pause game
- **Click** - Select Josh units on the canvas

### Mouse
- Click on Joshes to select them
- Use UI buttons for various actions

## 🌟 Core Features

### 1. Space Mining System
- Mine various types of space rocks:
  - **Common Rocks** - Basic resources
  - **Iron** - Used for crafting
  - **Titanium** - Advanced materials
  - **Platinum** - Rare materials
  - **Crystals** - High-tech components
  - **Antimatter** - Ultra-rare resource
- Auto-mining with upgraded technology
- Sector scanning to discover new rocks

### 2. Survival Mechanics (The Long Dark Inspired)
- **Health** - Your life force
- **Hunger** - Decreases over time, affects health
- **Temperature** - Stay warm in the cold void
- **Oxygen** - Critical for survival

### 3. Josh Management (AOE2 Inspired)
- Recruit multiple Josh cats
- Assign roles:
  - ⛏️ **Mining** - Gather resources
  - ⚔️ **Combat** - Fight enemies
  - 🔬 **Research** - Advance technology
- Level up Joshes through experience
- Train and upgrade your workforce

### 4. Fleet & Combat System
- Build various ship types:
  - 🚀 **Fighter** - Fast attack ship
  - ⛏️ **Mining Ship** - Resource gathering
  - 🛸 **Cruiser** - Heavy combat vessel
- Real-time space battles
- Projectile-based combat
- Defend against enemy AI factions

### 5. Territory Control (Monopoly Inspired)
- Capture and own sectors of space
- Upgrade territories for increased income
- Collect passive credits from territories
- Trade with AI opponents

### 6. Technology Tree (MOO2 Inspired)
Research advanced technologies:
- **Mining Tech** - Advanced/Quantum/Temporal Mining
- **Combat Tech** - Lasers/Plasma/Antimatter weapons
- **Defense Tech** - Energy/Phase shields
- **Economy Tech** - Trade networks and markets
- **Exploration Tech** - Advanced sensors and warp drive

### 7. Crafting System (Raft Inspired)
Craft powerful items:
- Mining Laser
- Advanced Space Suit
- Oxygen Tank
- Shield Generator
- Food Synthesizer
- Warp Core
- Defense Turret
- Resource Refinery

### 8. Pac-Man Mechanics
- Navigate through space maze
- Collect energy pellets for credits
- Power pellets restore health
- Ghost enemies chase you
- Eat scared ghosts for bonus credits

### 9. AI Opponents
Three rival factions:
- 🐕 **Evil Dog Empire** - Highly aggressive
- 🦅 **Bird Alliance** - Moderate threat
- 🤖 **Robot Collective** - Very hostile

AI opponents expand, build ships, and attack!

## 🎯 Pro Tips

1. **Manage Survival Stats** - Keep hunger, temp, and oxygen above 50%
2. **Automate Mining** - Research Advanced Mining tech for auto-mining
3. **Balance Your Joshes** - Mix miners, fighters, and researchers
4. **Expand Early** - More territories = more passive income
5. **Craft Strategically** - Prioritize oxygen tanks and food synthesizers
6. **Build Fleet** - Strong defense prevents costly damage
7. **Collect Pellets** - Easy credits and hunger restoration
8. **Avoid Ghosts** - Unless you have power pellets active
9. **Research First** - Tech advantages compound over time
10. **Trade Wisely** - Convert excess rocks to credits and food

## 🌌 Lore

In the distant future, cats named Josh have mastered space travel and mining technology. Their mission: extract valuable resources from asteroid fields across the galaxy. But they're not alone - rival factions compete for the same resources, and survival in space is a constant challenge.

As commander of the Josh Space Mining Corporation, you must balance resource gathering, territory expansion, technological advancement, and combat readiness. The fate of all the Joshes rests in your paws!
